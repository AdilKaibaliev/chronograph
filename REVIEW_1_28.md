# Frontier and discoverability review 1.28

## Historical corrections

- 622–629: replace the premature peninsula-wide early Islamic outline with Medina, western Arabian communities and the Khaybar stage. Mecca enters in 630. The 631–632 extent represents the political agreements described in the card, not uniformly administered provinces.
- Aksum: retain the African shore and remove the erroneous Yemeni extension throughout 610–632. Former Aksumite possessions in Yemen had passed to Sasanian authority before the atlas begins.
- Spania: date the loss of the remaining mainland Roman province to 625; retain North Africa. Keep the Visigothic outline north of the maritime separator. This is not a reconstruction of every town's individual surrender date.
- Qara Khitai: the Amu Darya regional edge and the separate Khwarazmian administration replace a double-filled strip. Khwarazm's tribute is recorded in words, not painted as direct occupation. Existing conquest changes from 1210 remain in place.

## Publication and search

The offline index.html remains self-contained and is the regression-test input. The release creates public/ with a small HTML document, ordered deferred scripts and content-addressed cached assets. Data round-trip verification compares every extracted value with the original source. All assets are local; no new third-party font, script or analytics dependency is introduced.

The atlas brand links to a guide. English, Russian and Kyrgyz guides each include eight regional reading pages with dated entries drawn from the same catalogue. A Chinese overview describes the available interface languages accurately. These pages provide real content and map links without requiring JavaScript.

Canonical URLs, reciprocal HTML hreflang, WebSite/CollectionPage structured data, descriptions, a social preview image, favicon, robots.txt and sitemap.xml are built and checked. Query-string map states canonicalize to the atlas root. The sitemap contains only canonical pages. Bibliographical citations remain plain text. IndexNow key is public by design and is not an account credential.

Search visibility requires crawler access and time; sitemap and IndexNow acceptance do not prove indexing or ranking. Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, Yandex Webmaster and Baidu Search Resource Platform ownership registration may require the owner's accounts. No fabricated verification tokens or guaranteed rankings.

Additional settlement control: the 624 Roman outline omitted Carthage/Tunis. Its African coastal core is restored through the early frames using Walter E. Kaegi, Muslim Expansion and Byzantine Collapse in North Africa (2010).
