cline-2.2.2.csv is the unmodified source download. Its SHA-256 appears in
registry.json. The atlas preserves source record IDs, dates, outcome categories
and non-exclusive typology flags. Country names are localized. Map coordinates
are added by the atlas as country-level reference anchors, derived from the
atlas's Natural Earth geography, with separate anchors for historic entities and
small islands. They are not event-site coordinates supplied by Cline Center.
The dataset's latest event date is 3 January 2026. This release does not claim a
complete inventory through the end of 2026. The source's classification is
attributed explicitly; curated stories have their own chronology and sources.
Registry import, localization and presentation modifications: Chronograph, 2026.

Detailed stories are original short summaries. Bibliography and research URLs
are in catalog.cjs. The public interface displays source names as plain text,
in accordance with the project owner's preference. No external source is used
to infer a precise troop route or a change of sovereign borders.
