# Regional shoreline extracts

The four windows in `harbours-141.json` were clipped and rounded by the Chronograph Atlas project from **GSHHG 2.3.7**, full-resolution shoreline polygons, by Paul Wessel (University of Hawai‘i) and Walter H. F. Smith (NOAA). The file contains readable geographic coordinates for areas around Copenhagen, Nuuk, Darwin and Punta Arenas. It is distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 3 or later. The accompanying files reproduce the original notice and applicable license texts.

The original binary distribution has SHA-256 `28600e8f7a08645aab43079326df6504212ec5ccb2b4bcf3b5f4f12ed60e82bc`. [GSHHG release and checksums](https://github.com/GenericMappingTools/gshhg-gmt/releases/tag/2.3.7).

The edits retain local land polygons, subtract lakes, restore islands and use the same result for shoreline clipping and the map background. No political boundary is inferred from a change of shoreline. Scholarly reference: Wessel and Smith, *A Global, Self-consistent, Hierarchical, High-resolution Shoreline Database*. [Original publication](https://doi.org/10.1029/96JB00104).
