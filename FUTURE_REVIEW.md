# Hadith atlas: quotations and geography — 2026-09-18

82 entries, four account sequences, 70 Arabic transmissions; Russian, English and Kyrgyz.

## Quotations

Each card displays an Arabic excerpt copied exactly from its referenced transmission and an original translation of its meaning in all three languages. The complete Arabic transmission is available below. Source collection and number accompany every excerpt. Narrator wording and embedded dialogue are distinguished from the Prophet's direct speech.

The five cards based on Bukhari 3176 now give the complete six-sign passage, including all six items and the final banner numbers. Muslim 2128b includes both categories mentioned in its opening. Bukhari 7132 includes the full encounter with the believer. The Constantinople excerpt includes Muslim 2897's statement that the first announcement of the Dajjal is false.

quotes.tsv stores excerpt search text and translations; quotes.cjs locates the exact passage in the original Arabic. Tests check coverage, reference association and exact original-text matches.

## Geography

geography.cjs separates historically established geography from places named in hadiths. It adds eight previously unmapped cards. Thirty-eight cards now have mapped locations; 44 retain an explicit world overview because this catalogue's cited evidence does not establish their location. No arbitrary pins or invented routes are added for those 44 cards.

- Kisra: al-Madain/Ctesiphon, treasury and capture in 637 CE / 16 AH. The shaded polygon is an original schematic of the mainland Sasanian core in the early seventh century, not the conquest frontier in 637 or the maximum extent in 620. Temporary conquests in Egypt and al-Sham are excluded. Peripheral possessions such as Yemen are not represented by this mainland-core polygon.
- Al-Hasan: Kufa, where al-Hasan and Muawiya personally confirmed the settlement in 661 CE / 41 AH. This is labelled the completion of reconciliation, not an asserted exact signing building.
- Ammar: the Siffin battle area on the right bank of the Euphrates between Raqqa and Balis, 657 CE / 37 AH. The highlight is an approximate area, not an identified spot of death or the modern mausoleum in Raqqa.
- Death of the Prophet: Medina, 632 CE / 11 AH.
- Prophetic mission: Mecca, city-level geography.
- Splitting of the moon: Mecca as the place of witnesses, supported by the additional geographical reference Bukhari 4867; no terrestrial location is assigned to the moon itself.
- Al-Rawha: approximate valley southwest of Medina on the route to Mecca.
- Tabala: approximate valley associated with Dhu al-Khalasa in Muslim 2906.

The camera includes every territory vertex when framing an event. A selected event has an explicit map caption; historical dates appear only as historical metadata. Old links containing the default empty fview=1,0,0 now use the event's fitted geography. Users can refocus with Show on map. Existing historical atlas datasets and Islamic nomenclature are unchanged.

## Source constraints and verification

Muslim 2897 supplies Medina → al-Amaq OR Dabiq → Constantinople → al-Sham. Lines are schematic directions. Abu Dawud 4292 supplies the joint campaign and unnamed meadow; it is not treated as a return from Constantinople. Abu Dawud 4284 concerns the Mahdi's descent from Fatimah and does not name a place. Both Abu Dawud reports retain the named sahih assessment by al-Albani. Weak reports remain excluded.

Validation checks all 82 records, three languages, sequence graph, Arabic excerpt fidelity, complete numbered passages, mapped historical dates, Sasanian polygon containment/exclusions, unknown-location handling and JavaScript syntax. Historical/i18n preservation, translation audit and support tests also run. Vercel regenerates index.html from source during deployment. Current public verification is recorded in PUBLICATION_CHECK.md in the local release package.
