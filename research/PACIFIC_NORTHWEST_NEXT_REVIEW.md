# Pacific Northwest: follow-up review

17 November 2026. These findings are not an implemented border correction.

- Vancouver Island is handled in VANCOUVER_ISLAND_REVIEW.md.
- San Juan Island must not inherit the Vancouver Island transfer: the 1846
  channel wording led to a separate dispute. National Park Service records
  the 1859 crisis, British Marines arriving on 21 March 1860, twelve years of
  joint military occupation, and the arbitration award on 21 October 1872.
  https://www.nps.gov/sajh/learn/historyculture/the-pig-war.htm
- The International Boundary Commission distinguishes the 1818 boundary east
  of the Rockies from the 1846 western extension. The current Louisiana
  envelope before 1818 must not be described as an already settled treaty line.
  A competing-claims reconstruction is needed before replacing it.
  https://www.internationalboundarycommission.org/en/about/history.php
- The Oregon regional profile retains its 1846 claims caption through modern
  years. It needs a separately researched sequence for the organized territory
  (1848), Washington separation (1853), and statehood (1859), retaining distinct
  Indigenous geography instead of treating the old regional extent as today's
  state of Oregon. This is not changed by the island correction.

No precise pre-1872 San Juan partition or pre-1818 Louisiana boundary is
invented here. These remain open, rather than being marked source-certified.
