# Harsha: display lifetime and southern frontier

The legacy profile ended at 632 although its own description stated 606–647.
Retain the political layer through 647. The former coarse envelope reached
21 degrees north across central India. Constrain the Deccan-facing section by
an approximate Narmada regional line, retaining Kannauj and land to its north.
The southern constraint uses the southern arc of Cliopatria v0.2.1's
612–622 reference (CC BY 4.0), stored with full feature and source checksum.
It is an upper-limit constraint on the old envelope, not a backdated conquest.
The northern source outline is rejected because it excludes Thanesar, the
dynasty's original centre. The earlier proposed meridian-limited mask was
rejected during visual review because its eastern end formed an artificial step.
The line is a regional reconstruction, not surveyed river geometry. No claim
is made here to reconstruct annual acquisitions in Bengal, Gujarat or Odisha.
