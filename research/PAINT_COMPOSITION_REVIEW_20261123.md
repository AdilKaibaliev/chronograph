# Fill composition review — 23 November 2026

The earlier annual survey checked independent political surfaces. It did not
cover all contextual tints, dependent territories, selected provinces or repeated
occupation hatches. A separate integer-polygon audit found 102 candidate pairs
outside the political surfaces. After accounting for the preceding release's
province-restoration masks, 100 pairs remained suitable regression cases.

The renderer now composites dependent territories by their dated owner, with
the same shade as that owner's primary surface. Existing political fills and
restored provinces take precedence. Different dependent owners do not receive
one another's overlapping area: their common part remains neutral. That neutral
paint appears only in the conflict, never underneath a translucent owner colour.
Nested dependencies follow the recorded hierarchy; occupation is not treated
as an ordinary change of sovereign parent.

Cultural and settlement regions share a contextual surface outside political
fills. Their overlapping records no longer mix country colours or darken one
another. Their outlines, names, markers and source-bearing profiles remain.
Occupation and resistance retain one shared hatch, including where a theatre
and a constituent province describe the same place. Selection of an existing
province or a member of a unified Mongol realm uses its outline rather than a
second political fill. Shared frontier bands apply opacity to their group once;
the same rule applies to the early chronology's shared bands.

The detailed Mongol view had a separate exemption that skipped member/member
intersection checks. It is removed. Before 1260, a displayed ulus is cut out of
the imperial parent's paint while retaining the ulus. Later overlapping ulus
reconstructions receive the existing neutral frontier symbol. The unified view
still uses the recorded combined realm. Historical source polygons, chronology,
ownership records and citations are not edited in this release.

Validation covers the complete annual political survey in both display modes,
the ancient boundary inventory, dated renderer tests and an independent SVG
point compositor at the confirmed regression locations. It checks opacity,
mask references, preservation of coverage and the absence of grey underpaint.
Offline SVG comparisons are diagnostic renders, not live browser screenshots.

Live browser inspection remains unavailable: automatic approval review rejected
window activation because the previous browser URL-verification restriction was
still active. No alternate browser-control route was used. This technical
review is not a certification of every historical frontier's source accuracy.
