The triage contains 155 flagged profiles. A flag is not proof of error, and
review of one segment does not certify the whole profile or all its dates.
The revised audit evaluates displayed imperial unions, not hidden component
polygons. No automatic decorative smoothing or new ownership is inferred.

  https://history.state.gov/milestones/1830-1860/oregon-territory

  *Foreign Relations of the United States, 1906*, Part I, document 642.
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1906p1/d642
