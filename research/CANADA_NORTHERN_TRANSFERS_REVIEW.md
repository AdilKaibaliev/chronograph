# Canada: northern mainland and Arctic transfer

Canadian Heritage, *Historical boundaries of Canada*, records the transfer of
Rupert's Land and the North-Western Territory in 1870, British Columbia's
accession in 1871, Prince Edward Island's in 1873, the transfer of the Arctic
Archipelago in 1880, the Alaska settlement in 1903, the Labrador decision in
1927 and Newfoundland's accession in 1949.

https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/services/historical-boundaries-canada.html

The displayed Canadian outline previously stopped at 60N through 1945. The
repair adds the northern continental land from 1870, excludes British Columbia
until 1871, and adds the northern islands from 1880. The imperial outline is
updated through 1918, while subsequent Dominion relationships remain intact.
Inuit cultural territories are retained independently of state sovereignty.

Geometry: existing Natural Earth Canadian reference coastline; public-domain
Natural Earth Admin 1 masks exclude British Columbia before accession and
Newfoundland/Labrador throughout this repair. The largest continental component
is retained to discard coastal slivers from the difference in source scales.
No CShapes coordinates are used. The reference does not resolve pre-1927
Labrador claims. The existing generalised Alaska coastal strip remains; the
1903 award is not represented as a new Canadian annexation.

The selectable Alaskan regional sketch is clipped to the same US reference
as its imperial parent from the 1867 transfer through 1945. This removes a
small duplicate fill along the Canadian edge; Canada and the British union
use the complementary US exclusion. The existing `sharedNorthernFrontier`
source records the 1825 coastal strip and 1903 award. It remains a generalised
coastal-strip representation before 1903, not a reconstruction of rival claims.

Scope: confirmed missing northern extent, not certification of every Canadian
border or colonial claim. Southern provincial sketches, Vancouver Island,
and a detailed reconstruction of competing Labrador and Alaska claims remain
separate historical/cartographic review items. Source-based uncertainty remains
visible; development-status language has been removed from visitor-facing text.

The 1870 transfer also included southwestern Baffin Island, as recorded in the
Northwest Territories Attorney General's Supreme Court factum (file 37398,
paragraph 1). A dated geographical event identifies this regional inclusion.
The internal watershed limit on Baffin Island is not invented as a precise
political polygon; the whole archipelago is coloured from the 1880 transfer
of remaining British rights. This is not a claim that every island first
entered Canada in 1880.
https://scc-csc.gc.ca/pdf/case-documents/37398/FM070_Intervener_Attorney-General-of-Northwest-Territories.pdf

Validation: annual presence of Whitehorse, Yellowknife, northern Manitoba and
northern Quebec from 1870; Arctic test locations from 1880; BC exclusion in
1870 and inclusion in 1871; Newfoundland excluded through 1948 and included in
1949; no Canadian coverage at Alaska/Greenland control points; imperial union
and the 1945/1946 transition.
